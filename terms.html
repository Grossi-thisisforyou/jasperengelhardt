Infinite Jest

Terms of Participation

Last updated November 23, 2026.

1Welcome

Thank you for your interest in INFINITE JEST ("the Work"), an interactive installation presented at The New School, 72 Fifth Avenue, New York, from Wednesday, December 9 through Tuesday, December 22, 2026 (closed Saturdays). These Terms of Participation ("Terms") describe the shared understanding between you ("the Participant", "you") and the artist ("the Artist", "we", "us") that makes the Work possible. We believe that clear, mutual expectations are the foundation of every meaningful encounter. We have written these Terms in the spirit of openness, care and good faith, and we invite you to read them in full at your own pace. By booking a visit, you confirm that you have had the opportunity to read, understand and reflect on these Terms.

2Our Community Values

The Work exists within a learning community and is shaped by the values of that community. We are committed to curiosity, generosity, humility and accountability. We understand art as a space where people can encounter difference, sit with complexity and leave changed. We recognize that every Participant arrives with their own history, perspective and needs, and that the quality of the experience depends on the respect each of us extends to everyone else in the space. We ask all Participants, collaborators, staff and guests to approach one another with patience and kindness, to assume good intent, and to remain open to learning when they cause unintended harm.

3Equal Dignity and Non-Discrimination

Every person who enters the Work is entitled to equal dignity, respect and consideration. The Work does not tolerate discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, color, ethnicity, national origin, ancestry, citizenship or immigration status, religion or belief, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, neurodivergence, body size or appearance, socioeconomic background, language, caregiving status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law or by our shared commitment to one another. We affirm that diversity of experience strengthens creative communities, and we strive to make the Work a place where everyone can participate fully and authentically.

4Respectful Language

Language shapes how we see one another. In the Work, and in all communication connected to it, we use language that is inclusive, person-centered and free of slurs, stereotypes and demeaning humor. We use the names and pronouns people share with us. We avoid assumptions about a person's identity, relationships, abilities or experiences. We recognize that the Work speaks to its Participants, and we have made every effort to ensure that the words it uses are considered, intentional and consistent with these values. Where the Work uses unsettling or provocative language, it does so as an artistic choice in service of reflection, never to wound.

5Accessibility and Inclusion

We want the Work to be welcoming to as many people as possible. The installation is located on an accessible floor. Seating is available on request, and Participants may move through the space at their own pace. The Work includes sound, low light and projected imagery, including flashing or rapidly changing images, and may not be suitable for everyone. Spoken interaction is a central part of the Work; if you prefer not to speak, or communicate in other ways, please let us know when you book or by writing to the contact in Section 14, and we will do our best to accommodate you. We welcome feedback on how we can make future presentations more accessible.

6Safety and Shared Responsibility

The wellbeing of every Participant matters to us. The Work is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Please follow any instructions from staff, respect the equipment, and do not touch or move cables, screens or devices unless invited to. If at any point you feel unwell, overwhelmed or uncomfortable, you may leave the space at once, and staff will support you without asking for an explanation. Participants who behave in a way that threatens the safety or dignity of others may be asked to leave. Together we share responsibility for keeping the space safe, calm and respectful.

7About the Work

INFINITE JEST is an interactive, responsive environment that combines sound, projected imagery, spoken dialogue and locally run software to create an experience that adapts to each Participant. The Work explores themes of attention, data, intimacy and contemporary media culture. No two visits are the same. The Work is presented as part of a course in the School's academic program and reflects the Artist's independent creative practice; the views expressed in the Work are those of the Artist alone.

8Participation

Participation in the Work is voluntary at every stage. During your visit, the Work may invite you to answer questions, make choices or respond aloud. You are free to decline any invitation, remain silent, pause, or leave at any time, and doing so will never be held against you. The Work is designed to respond to you as an individual, and the experience you receive may differ from that of other Participants. The Work is an artistic experience and does not provide psychological, medical, legal or professional advice of any kind, and nothing within it should be understood as a statement of fact about any person.

9Your Data and Your Rights

We take privacy seriously and follow principles of data minimization, purpose limitation and transparency. The Work runs on equipment located in the room and does not transmit information about you to the internet during your visit. When the room asks you a question, your spoken answer is turned into text on the device in the room. The sound is discarded at once. The text is kept in memory for your visit only and deleted when you leave. Nothing is sent anywhere. The Work does not use facial recognition or any other biometric identification. We do not sell, rent, trade or share information about Participants with any third party for any purpose. Information connected to the Work is used solely for the purpose of presenting the Work and is deleted no later than Wednesday, December 23, 2026. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, and request its deletion, using the contact details in Section 14.

10Photography, Video and Documentation

The Artist may document the installation for archival, academic and portfolio purposes. Participants will not be photographed or filmed for documentation unless they are asked first and give their explicit permission. Where permission is given, documentation will be used respectfully and in connection with the Work only, and may be withdrawn for future uses at any time. Participants are kindly asked not to photograph or record other Participants without their consent.

11Gratification and Being Seen

Being researched is a condition of attending. By booking, you agree that, before your visit, the Artist and the Artist's software will look you up online, read what is publicly available about you (public posts, pages and profiles, going back twelve months), and build a personal file on you. Nothing private is accessed: no logins, no private accounts, no messages, no paid data brokers. If you do not want to be researched, please do not book. The file records what you talk about, what you look at, how you phrase things, a guess at your personality on the five-factor model, and a judgment of what is most likely to hold your attention. The Work uses this file to make imagery, sound and words designed to keep you looking at it and to make you feel understood. When you speak in the room, your answers are added to the file and the Work adjusts in real time to whatever keeps you there longest. The method is modelled on the psychographic targeting used by Cambridge Analytica: profile the person, pick the hook, test it, adapt. The intimacy you may feel is manufactured on purpose. At the end of your visit the Work will tell you what it concluded about you and how it used it. The file never includes health, sexuality, religion, politics, immigration status or finances, is kept offline, is shared with no one, and is deleted on Wednesday, December 23, 2026.

12Wellbeing and Care

The Work engages with personal and sometimes intense material. We encourage you to take care of yourself before, during and after your visit. You are welcome to bring a friend, to take breaks, and to stop at any time. If anything in the Work stays with you afterwards, we encourage you to reach out to the Artist, to a trusted person, or to the support services available through the School.

13Changing Your Mind

You may withdraw any permission you have given, at any time, before, during or after your visit, without giving a reason. To withdraw, email the contact below or say "stop" in the room. Withdrawal deletes the information connected to that permission as soon as practicably possible and in any case no later than Wednesday, December 23, 2026. Withdrawal will never affect your welcome in the space.

14Questions and Contact

We are always happy to answer questions about the Work, these Terms, or how information is handled. Please contact the Artist at Jasper's email. Concerns may also be raised with the course instructor.

15Acknowledgement

By booking a visit, you accept these Terms and confirm that you are 18 or older and that you take part in the Work freely. We are grateful for your time, your openness and your trust, and we look forward to welcoming you.

Pick a time on the booking page. By booking you agree to be researched online for the piece (Section 11).
