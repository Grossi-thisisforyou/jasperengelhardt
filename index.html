Ruben

Ruben · Museum

Own work

Work that starts from research and from machines: a family archive, recorded conversations, small computers and language models that read the room. Made for the black box, the page and the classroom.

AI-automated performance · 2026, in development

Infinite Jest

A performance about making the performer obsolete. After David Foster Wallace’s novel, whose film is so entertaining that no one can look away, a machine takes over the show: a 2009 Nokia N900 becomes a haptic controller for up to eight channels of video projection, while a Raspberry Pi 5 on stage listens, reacts and re-cues the projections in real time, working with large language models. The system reads the room, the day and the audience, so no two performances are alike. Developed in David Carroll’s course Small Linux Devices, Large Language Models at Parsons School of Design, in open collaboration with AI.

Solo performance · The New School, April 2026

Berghein

A solo with live projection: the Furka Pass on film, a grandfather’s front-line letters from the Second World War, archive documents, and the news of present wars. Built from the same archive as Never Again. Next Time. Shown in Solo Together, 151 Bank Street, New York, 17 and 18 April 2026.

Director · The New School, 7 May 2026

The Seagull, New York, 2029

Chekhov’s The Seagull, moved to New York three years from now. With Aster Drewe, Shannon Stowe, Magdi Hazaa and Bryce Hayden Henry. Sound by Catherine Ashley, souffleuse Rebecca Berger. Advanced Directing, The New School.

Readings · GRIMMWELT Kassel, 2026–27

Reading Helga

For Reshaping Tales | Helga Gebert (Zwischen Gestalten), the first comprehensive solo exhibition of his grandmother’s work, he recorded the stories for the reading corner of GRIMMWELT Kassel: Grimm tales in their 1857 versions, from Aschenputtel to Frau Holle, and Helga Gebert’s own retellings, from Die Seejungfrau to Igel und Maus. Visitors can listen to them in the museum until 11 April 2027.

Writing and performance · 2026, in progress

Never Again. Next Time.

Prose and solo performance on a German family’s war history, built from recorded interviews with relatives in their nineties and more than five hundred pages of front-line letters. Research in Romania, summer 2026.

Participatory theatre · in development

Echoes Across Oceans

Youth theatre on climate justice, linking Neukölln in Berlin with Chinatown and Two Bridges in New York.

Short biography · 100 words

Jasper Engelhardt is a performance artist and actor based in Berlin and New York. Trained as an actor at Zurich University of the Arts, he has played leads for ARD, Netflix and BR, and was nominated for the Deutscher Schauspielpreis as Best Newcomer in 2019. He reperformed Marina Abramović’s Imponderabilia, Luminosity and Nude with Skeleton in her retrospective at Kunsthaus Zürich, and performs in her Balkan Erotic Epic at Park Avenue Armory in December 2026. His own work spans performance, directing, readings and writing. He is an MFA candidate at The New School and a DAAD scholar.

Long biography · 250 words

Jasper Engelhardt is a performance artist and actor based in Berlin and New York. He trained as an actor at Zurich University of the Arts and spent his twenties on screen and stage: a lead as Farim in Jan Bonny’s Polizeiruf 110, which brought a Deutscher Schauspielpreis nomination for Best Newcomer in 2019, Hänsel in Netflix’s The Grimm Reality, and Franz Kafka and Thomas Bernhard at Theater der Jugend Wien. He has worked with Wu Tsang at Schauspielhaus Zürich and with Charlotte Brathwaite at La MaMa in New York.

In museums he performs durational, body-based work. He reperformed Marina Abramović’s Imponderabilia, Luminosity and Nude with Skeleton in her retrospective at Kunsthaus Zürich, and performs in her Balkan Erotic Epic at Park Avenue Armory in December 2026. Earlier he performed in works by Florentina Holzinger and, at Manifesta 11, Michel Kessler.

His own work spans performance, directing, readings and writing. The solo Berghein (New York, 2026) worked with projection, film and a family archive. Infinite Jest, in development, hands a performance over to a machine that reads the room. His readings of Grimm tales and of his grandmother Helga Gebert’s stories play at GRIMMWELT Kassel until April 2027.

He is an MFA candidate in Contemporary Theatre and Performance at The New School, supported by the DAAD, and assists the curator Klaus Biesenbach.
